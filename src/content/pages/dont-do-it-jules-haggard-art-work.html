DON'T DO IT

To anyone who’s had a stray dark thought enter their mind while hiking, DON’T DO IT explores what we owe one another in the wilderness. In what first looks like digital mock-ups but is in fact huge tarps adorned with graphic symbols captured by drone photography, onlookers are told to: resist pushing people off bridges; refrain from starting forest fires; ban dogs from lake public lakes; and other intriguing restrictions. Will you listen? Or do laws get lost in the woods, too?
