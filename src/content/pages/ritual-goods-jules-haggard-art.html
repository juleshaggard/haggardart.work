HAGGARD

RITUAL GOODS

Anything with legs can walk. With RITUAL GOODS, I craft wooden objects intended for rituals in the home—cutting boards, bowls, benches—but bestow them with a feral energy that suggests they might crawl right out the front door. Bringing the outside in, bringing the inside out, these warm handicrafts add new life to those who host them.
