HAGGARD

COLLAPSE

What if you could peel back the universe to see the graph paper that keeps everything in its proper place? COLLAPSE is a series that explores pattern, geometry, and hidden structures. Utilizing spheres, folds, sine waves, and Gaussian noise, these works of pointillism contrast the pleasing order and repetition of dots with the inherent chaos of the actual living, breathing world.
