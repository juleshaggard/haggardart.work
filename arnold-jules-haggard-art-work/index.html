ARNOLD

Arnold is a beautiful, sleepy town smack dab in the middle of the California Sierras. In ARNOLD I want to show the little moments of community and joy that bring this town together. The work below is currently comprised of photos from the annual Logging Jamboree in White Pines, CA.
